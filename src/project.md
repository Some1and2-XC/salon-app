# Project

Primary requirements: An app that allow the customer to book appointments so the owner to see and schedule appointments. When customers book appointments, the app will send the unbook appointment into the app with the time slot, appointments detail like: services name, the time will take, the time customers want to come in.

Features:

1. The ability to see the schedule day by day
2. There should be a login page for the owner so they can see the information for this nail salon. They have two locations so they can log in two different account for 2 strore
2. The app will let the owner to set the time of open and close(ex: we open at 10AM and 7PM so the customer can book at available time if there is open time slot)
2. The appointment send from customers will show up as red as it not yet confirmed by the owner yet. It will be white if the owner accept the appointment
2. The main page will have the time slot grid and ability to book by simple click on the open space
2. When book an appointment for the customer, the owner will be able to select the date, time, and service. Addition, there will be an open space to edit appointment note for customer.
2. A webpage for the customer to book
2. Send notification to customer when the appointment is confirm, it also send notification the day before the appointment date to remind customers to come in time
2. There should be ability to add service to the service’s list, the list will show up when the owner or customers book. The service list will have the service name, price and the time need to complete service
2. The ability to move and drag the service appointment for organize and reschedule
2. Ability to store customer name and phone number for the future. Store customer’s history
